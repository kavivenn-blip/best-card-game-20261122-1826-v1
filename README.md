**Monsoon Market** — a 48-card spice-merchant card duel: 4 spice families, combo chains, and a rival whose plans you can read. You run a market stall against Mara, a rival merchant. Play chili to burn, turmeric to earn, cardamom to shield, star anise to trick. Two cards of one spice double the second; three call a Monsoon Surge. Mara announces her plan before she acts — answer it or pay for it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a card to play it (or keys 1–5), Enter ends your turn, R restarts, M mutes. No network, no build step — the game is plain static files under `game/`.
